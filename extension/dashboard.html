Scrape a Sales Navigator search

  1. Open your lead search in Sales Navigator.
  2. Click the ReplyTrack icon at the top right of Chrome — on a Sales Navigator page it opens the scraper.
  3. Set the number of pages, pick Quick, Standard or Deep Run, and press Start Auto-Scraping.
  4. Every lead goes into the SCRAP tab of your sheet by itself — email, phone and the rest are filled in as they're found.

Email & phone

  1. One person: open their LinkedIn profile, click the ReplyTrack icon, then Find contact info.
  2. Everyone in a search: pick Deep Run — it finds email and phone for each lead and writes them into SCRAP.
  3. Tick Also read the banner image to read contact details written in the profile's banner (OCR).

The scraper uses LinkedIn from this browser like outreach does. Don't run a big scrape and an outreach run at the same time — too many requests can lead to restrictions on your LinkedIn account. “Pause everything” doesn't stop the scraper: use its own Stop button.
